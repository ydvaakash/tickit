## Data Collected

We collect, store, and process the following user data as part of providing the services of **Tickit**:

### 1. Account Information

- Email Address
- Password (stored in encrypted form)
- Full Name
- Country
- Time Zone

### 2. Task Information

- Todo task details created by users, including but not limited to task titles, descriptions, due dates, statuses, and any other information voluntarily provided by the user in the form of a task.

### 3. Device and Connection Information

- Browser Details
- IP Address (for authentication, security monitoring, and fraud detection purposes)

## Purpose of Data Collection

The data collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

- To provide core features of **Tickit**.
- To authenticate and secure user accounts.
- To personalize user experience.
- To monitor and prevent unauthorized or suspicious activities.
- To analyze behavioral patterns and usage statistics for product improvements.
- To perform internal product research and development.
- To enforce compliance with this Privacy Policy, **Terms of Service**, and any other legal obligations.

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to any third party under any circumstances.

## Data Retention

We retain the collected data only for as long as it is necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable laws.

The retention periods will be as follows:


| **Data Type** | **Retention Period** |
| :--------- | :---------------- |
| Account Information | As long as the account is active, or until the user requests data deletion |
| Task Information | As long as the user account is active or until manually deleted by the user |
| Device and Connection Data | Up to **30 days** for security monitoring purposes |


After the retention period expires, your data will be **permanently deleted** from our databases, except for any backups or logs that may be stored solely for security, compliance, or dispute resolution purposes.
